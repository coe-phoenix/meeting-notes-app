Recording consent. You may only upload or record audio of people who have
      consented to being recorded. You are responsible for obtaining that consent under Malaysia's
      PDPA and any other applicable law. Do not upload audio you have no right to record.

      
What we store, and for how long. We store your audio, transcripts, and
      summaries so you can access and download them. Files are auto-deleted after the retention
      window shown in the app, and you can delete any recording — or your whole account — at any time.

      
Processing. Audio is sent to third-party speech-to-text and AI providers
      to produce transcripts and summaries. At rest, content is encrypted where enabled.

      
Acceptable use. No unlawful, harassing, or infringing content. We may
      suspend accounts and remove content that is reported and found to violate these terms.

      
Report abuse. Use the “Report content” action on any recording, or contact
      the operator. We aim to review reports promptly and remove violating content.
